Input rules and troubleshooting
  • Use correct element capitalization: Co is cobalt; CO is carbon monoxide.
  • Use + between species and exactly one reaction arrow.
  • Write subscripts as normal digits: H2O, not H₂O (plain digits are most reliable).
  • Wrap polyatomic groups in parentheses and add counts after the closing bracket.
  • For ionic reactions, include charge notation consistently. Balancing checks atom and net-charge conservation, not whether a reaction is chemically feasible.
Custom input accepts formulas, nested brackets, hydrate dots, leading coefficients, and supported ionic charge notation.
